Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 27, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the policies of Flex Notes regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information Collection and Use

Flex Notes is built as a local-first, offline application.

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, location, or device identifier).
  • On-Device Storage: All note contents, categories, settings, and other app data are stored exclusively on your device's internal storage sandbox. We do not have access to your note content or files.
  • No Network Operations: The app functions entirely offline. None of your data is transmitted to servers, cloud-databases, or external locations.

3. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

Flex Notes does not integrate any third-party SDKs, ads, analytics tools, or tracking trackers. Your use of the application is completely isolated to your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any data. Accordingly, we do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please be assured that the application does not have the capability to collect or transmit such data.

5. Data Security

Because your notes are stored on your local device, security depends entirely on your own device's security controls (such as PIN, passwords, fingerprints, and device encryption). We encourage you to configure standard security settings on your device to prevent unauthorized access.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this Privacy Policy page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the developer contact email listed on the Google Play Store store page.
