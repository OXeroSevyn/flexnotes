Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Flex Notes. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information in our application.

2. Information Collection and Use

Flex Notes is built as a local-first application.

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information (such as your name, email, phone number, or location).
  • Local Storage Only: All note entries, configurations, and application data are saved locally on your device storage. We have no access to your notes or private files.
  • No Network Communication: The app operates strictly offline. No note content is uploaded to any servers, cloud environments, or external databases.

3. Third-Party Services

Flex Notes does not integrate any third-party SDKs, ad networks, tracking technologies, or analytical frameworks. Your usage remains entirely private and untracked.

4. Data Security

Since your notes are stored locally on your own device, the security of your data depends on your device's security configurations (such as screen locks, device encryption, and application permission management). We recommend securing your device to prevent unauthorized physical access to your notes.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page, and the effective date will be updated accordingly.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via your developer contact channel on the Google Play Store.
