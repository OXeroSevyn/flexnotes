🛡️ Privacy Policy

Because your trust is our priority, Flex Notes enforces a transparent, local-first policy regarding how your data is handled:

  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not ask for, gather, store, or share any personal registration data (such as emails, names, phone numbers, or account profiles).
  • Strictly Local Storage: All files, notes, settings, and database configurations remain inside your device's secure app sandbox. We have no backend server and no access to your documents.
  • Local Backups: The app supports generating local JSON backup files. These backups are stored in your device storage and are completely managed by you.
  • No Third-Party Access: We do not integrate tracking analytics, advertisement networks, or user profiling SDKs. Your app stays clean, light, and private.

🔐 Data Security & Safety

Security is a shared responsibility. Because Flex Notes stores your information locally on your device, we recommend using standard device locks (fingerprint, face unlock, PIN) and using built-in system encryption to protect your device from unauthorized physical access.
